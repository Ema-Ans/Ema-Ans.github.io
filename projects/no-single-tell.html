No Single Tell

Visible behavior during extended decision-making in elite chess.

How much of thinking can we actually see?

We study how visible behavior unfolds while expert chess players make extended decisions. The project aligns long-form chess video with human behavioral annotations, game information, clocks, and broad engine-derived decision context.

A human-centered measurement workflow

I also developed the behavioral taxonomy and reviewer workflow used by the study. While the paper is under review, the interface details, taxonomy, analyses, and results are intentionally staying off this page.

hci angle

The measurement question underneath the model

Rather than asking whether computation can directly infer an internal state from visible behavior, this work asks a more basic measurement question: what can human observations of behavior actually support?

The project treats behavior as situated and temporally evolving. It also studies the measurement process itself: what is observable, how humans code it, and how those observations can be aligned with context.
