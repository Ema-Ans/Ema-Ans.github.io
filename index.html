COMP6211J FINAL REPORT · PROPOSED METHOD + EXPERIMENTAL PLAN

Spectral Adaptive FLORA

Memory-efficient fine-tuning under annoyingly small GPU budgets.

Mapped the literature from adapters and LoRA through GaLore and FLORA, then proposed SA-FLORA: an extension that responds when the gradient subspace drifts, allocates rank across wavelet scales, and regularizes compressed gradients to preserve spectral structure.

The report defines the method, hypotheses, and evaluation plan; it does not present SA-FLORA as an experimentally validated result.
